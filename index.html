HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Are online classes available?

Yes! We offer online classes for students who prefer to learn from home.

Are offline classes available?

Yes! Offline classes are also available at our teaching location in KTC Nagar, Tirunelveli.

Who can join phonics classes?

Children who want to develop their English letter-sound knowledge, reading, and spelling skills can enquire. Class placement depends on the child's current learning level.

Do you offer Tamil Basic Classes?

Yes! We offer Tamil Basic Classes covering letter recognition, reading, writing, spelling, and grammar.

How can I enrol my child?

Contact us through WhatsApp to enquire about course fees, available timings, and admission procedures.
